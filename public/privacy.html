Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

This policy explains what MarginPilot collects and why.

What we collect

  • Account: your email and a hashed password (stored by our database provider, Supabase).
  • Store data you connect: suppliers you add and, once connected, your eBay listings and orders, used only to run the automations you turn on.
  • Payments: handled by Whop. We receive your plan and subscription status, not your card details.
  • AI usage: the text you send to AI research and the cost of each request, so we can apply your plan’s AI allowance. Requests are processed by Anthropic’s Claude API.

How we use it

To provide the service, bill your plan through Whop, apply AI allowances, and contact you about your account. We do not sell your data.

Your choices

Email us to access or delete your data, or delete your account.
